# Proposal

## Why

On the route-detail page the current-location button only toggles its highlight, and the live map doesn't show where the rider is. The stop timeline lists the whole line from its first terminal even though the rider boards at their nearest station. It also says nothing about connections, such as the Suffolk County Transit 56 at Smithtown or the subway at Penn Station and Woodside.

## What Changes

- **Location:**
  - The live route map shows the rider's location.
  - The route-detail location button refreshes the location and centers the live map on it, as the home button does.
  - Panning clears the button's selected state.
- **Timeline:** for the live route, the stop list starts at the rider's nearest station and runs to the end of the line in the selected direction.
- **Transfers:**
  - Each station row shows chips for transfer lines: other LIRR branches, subway, and buses within about 400 m. Chips use the agency's route colors and are capped at eight plus `+N`.
  - Data comes from the official static GTFS feeds of LIRR, Suffolk County Transit, NICE Bus, NYC Subway, and MTA Bus (Manhattan, Queens, and MTA Bus Company).
  - The data is generated by the new `scripts/generate_station_transfers.py` into `mobile/src/data/stationTransfers.ts`. Only the generated file and the script are committed; the feeds are downloaded at generation time.

## Impact

- **Code**:
  - `mobile/src/components/HomeScreen.tsx`: passes location and refresh to the route detail.
  - `mobile/src/components/RouteDetailView.tsx`: location button, timeline slice, and transfer chips.
  - `mobile/src/components/LirrRouteMap.native.tsx` and `.web.tsx`: user location marker and center-on-user.
  - New `mobile/src/data/stationTransfers.ts` (generated) and `scripts/generate_station_transfers.py`.
- **Data**: about 37 MB of feeds are downloaded only when regenerating. The generated TS file is about 185 lines.
- **Not covered**: Amtrak, NJ Transit, and PATH at Penn Station (their feeds aren't included). Bus and subway transfers for illustrated (non-live) routes.
- **Depends on**: `live-trains-and-nearest-stop` (nearest station and `liveStop`).
